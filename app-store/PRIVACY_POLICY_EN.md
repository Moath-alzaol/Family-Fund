# Family Fund Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026

Family Fund respects your privacy. This policy explains the data the app needs to provide its features and how that data is used and protected.

## Data we process

The app may process:

- Your name, username, and account identifier.
- Your role within the family group and monthly commitment amount.
- Balances, contributions, expenses, deposit or withdrawal requests, and related decisions.
- Notes and beneficiary names entered in requests.
- Read status for request-related notifications.

The app does not request bank account or payment-card details, connect to banks, or transfer real money.

## How we use data

Data is used only to provide app functionality, including authentication, balances and commitments, request creation and review, ledger and report generation, and notifications. We do not use the data for advertising or marketing, sell it, or use it to track you across apps or websites.

## Data sharing

Financial records and notes are visible to authorized members of the family group according to app permissions. Family Fund uses Supabase to host the database, provide authentication, and operate backend services. Supabase may process data on our behalf to provide those services under the arrangements and policies applicable to that service.

We do not share data with data brokers or advertising networks.

## Storage and security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and access is restricted at the database level. We retain data while the account or family group needs it, or as necessary to provide the service and comply with applicable legal requirements.

## Notifications

The app may request permission to display local notifications about requests and decisions. You can disable notifications in device settings. Notifications are not used for advertising.

## Your choices and rights

You can request access to or correction of your data using the contact method below. You can start deletion of your account and personal data directly in the app under **Settings → Delete Account**. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Shared financial records may be retained after your identity is removed when necessary to preserve the accuracy of the family ledger or comply with legal requirements.

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and is not submitted as a Made for Kids app.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app functionality or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when a new policy is published.
