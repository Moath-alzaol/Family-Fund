## English localization

### Name

Family Fund

### Subtitle

Transparent family budgeting

### Promotional text

Track contributions, commitments, balances, and shared expenses in one private place built for the whole family.

### Description

Family Fund is a private space that helps families manage a shared budget with clarity and transparency.

View the shared fund balance, monthly contributions, personal balances, and financial requests from one simple dashboard. Each member can see what has been paid and what remains due, while the administrator can review requests, approve or reject them, and maintain a clear record of every entry.

Key features:

• Shared fund balance and monthly collection progress
• Monthly commitment tracking for every member
• Personal deposit and withdrawal records
• Monthly contribution transfers into the family fund
• Shared-expense requests and review workflow
• Approvals and rejections with clear reasons
• Organized personal and shared-fund activity history
• Monthly contribution, expense, and net-change reports
• Notifications for new requests and decisions
• Shareable balance summaries
• Full Arabic and English interfaces

Family Fund does not connect to bank accounts or move real money. It records and organizes transactions completed outside the app so authorized family members always have a clear, shared view.

budget,expenses,contributions,balances,commitments,household,ledger,transparency

### What's New — Version 1.0

The first release of Family Fund brings shared contributions, commitment tracking, personal balances, approval workflows, monthly reports, notifications, and full Arabic and English support.

### Review notes

Family Fund is a private ledger for an invited family group. It does not connect to a bank, process payments, or transfer real money. Amounts entered in the app record transactions completed outside the app.

Please use the supplied admin demo account for the complete review. The Home screen shows the shared fund balance, monthly collection progress, member commitments, personal balance, and pending requests. Use “New Request” to record a deposit, withdrawal, contribution, or family expense. Member requests remain pending until the admin approves or rejects them. The Fund tab contains the activity ledger and monthly report. The Requests tab shows all statuses. Notifications are generated when requests are created or decided.

A second member demo account is listed above so the reviewer can verify both sides of the approval workflow. The demo accounts do not expire and contain sample data only.

This app is intended for Unlisted App distribution to a limited audience. An unlisted distribution request will be submitted after the app is sent to App Review.
